# AGENTS.md

## Repository Guidelines

- **Issue tracker**: Track issues, specs, and sub-issues with `gh`. Consult [`docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`](docs/agents/issue-tracker.md).
- **Triage labels**: Apply canonical roles (`ready-for-agent`, `needs-triage`, `needs-info`, `ready-for-human`, `wontfix`). Consult [`docs/agents/triage-labels.md`](docs/agents/triage-labels.md).
- **Domain vocabulary**: Follow glossary terms and recorded architectural decisions before naming concepts. Consult [`docs/agents/domain.md`](docs/agents/domain.md).
- **Architecture**: Structure systems into deep modules across explicit two-adapter seams. Consult [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Coding standards**: Design minimal interfaces, test observable outcomes through seams, and maintain zero plaintext at rest. Consult [`docs/CODING_STANDARDS.md`](docs/CODING_STANDARDS.md).
